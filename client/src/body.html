A HORSE TROUGH, AN ENAMEL KILN

The bath began as an act of nerve.

When John Michael Kohler looked at a cast iron horse trough in 1873, he saw the bathtub that would launch a global plumbing company. Kohler Co. was co-founded that year with the purchase of the Sheboygan Union Iron and Steel Foundry, and early products were farm implements, furniture castings and ornamental ironwork. The bathroom, it turned out, was a side effect of curiosity.

That instinct still runs the place. Kohler remains privately held and family-led, headquartered at 444 Highland Drive in Kohler, Wisconsin, with David Kohler as chair and chief executive — the fourth generation of Kohler family leadership and only the ninth person to lead the company.

1873

A foundry changes hands

Austrian immigrant John Michael Kohler and Charles Silberzahn buy the foundry from Kohler's father-in-law, Jacob Vollrath, for $5,000.

1883

Enamel meets iron

The breakthrough arrives when enamel is applied to a cast-iron horse trough — the first Kohler bath, and the start of a category.

TODAY

Beyond the bathroom

Plumbing fixtures, tile, furniture and cabinetry, engines and generators, alongside two of the world's finest five-star hospitality and golf destinations in Kohler, Wisconsin and St Andrews, Scotland.

2023

Come All Creators

The 150th anniversary is marked worldwide with limited-edition releases and a commemorative creative identity conceived with American artist Daniel Arsham.

What the designer weighs before it draws a line.

Every generated layout is measured on four things that decide whether a bathroom works: whether everything fits, whether each fixture has room to use, whether it holds your budget, and — if you switch it on — where it stands against Vastu guidance. The formula is shown beside every score.

Space fit

Clearances, door swing and wet-zone separation are checked against your exact length and width. Every fixture is placed only where its clearances hold.

Budget discipline

Fixtures are selected to land inside your range, not above it. The running total and what's left of your budget update as you change your mind.

Vastu guidance

Optional. Tell the designer which way your back wall faces and it rates each fixture against a traditional orientation table, and reports plainly where a fixture could not be placed in a preferred zone.
